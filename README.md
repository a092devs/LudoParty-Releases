# LudoParty — Official Releases

Official APK releases and downloads for **LudoParty**, the 2 to 8 player Ludo & Snakes & Ladders game for Android.

## Key Features
- **2 to 8 Players**: Classic 4-player cross board plus dynamic 5, 6, 7, and 8-player polygonal arenas.
- **Snakes & Ladders Mode**: Built-in Snakes & Ladders board mode alongside classic Ludo.
- **3D Upright Pawns & Isometric Dice**: Multi-face 3D tumbling dice and sculpted upright pawns.
- **15 Animated Dice Themes**: Unlock and equip 15 distinct dice themes in the Dice Shop.
- **26 Vector Avatars + Custom Photos**: Choose from 26 built-in vector avatars, upload a device photo, or sync your Google profile photo.
- **Google Sign-In & Cloud Leaderboard**: One-tap Google Sign-In (Android Credential Manager + Firebase Auth) plus username/password accounts.
- **Online Multiplayer & Voice Chat**: Quick Match, Private Rooms, and Agora voice chat.

## Downloads and Installation
Download the latest APK asset from the **[Releases](https://github.com/a092devs/LudoParty-Releases/releases)** page:

1. Download `LudoParty-v1.0.0-release.apk` from the latest release.
2. On your Android device, tap the file and select **Install** (allow *Install from Unknown Sources* if prompted).
3. Open **LudoParty** and roll the dice!

## Minimum Requirements
- **OS**: Android 7.0 (API level 24) or higher.
- **RAM**: 2 GB minimum (4 GB recommended).
- **Supported ABIs**: `arm64-v8a`, `armeabi-v7a`, `x86`, `x86_64`.
